Our Company Profile

Trusted Manufacturer & Global Exporter of Premium Wooden Safety Matches

Bell Match is a trusted safety match manufacturer built on quality, safety, innovation and customer commitment. As part of the Bell Group of Companies, with interests spanning engineering, hospitality and pyrotechnics, we bring strong expertise and responsible business practices to everything we do. What sets us apart is our ability to understand customer requirements and deliver reliable, flexible and customized solutions. Backed by a sustainable manufacturing approach, we focus on consistent quality while meeting the needs of diverse markets. Our 5-acre manufacturing facility, modern machinery and skilled workforce enable us to combine technology with manufacturing expertise at every stage. With a strong focus on safety, compliance and dependable service, Bell Match has built lasting customer relationships and a growing presence in international markets. As an established match manufacturer (decades of experience), Bell Match continues to develop its manufacturing capabilities to serve international buyers. Today, our footprint extends across 25+ countries, connecting Bell Match with customers worldwide. At Bell Match, service meets commitment, safety meets innovation, and partnerships are built to last.
