Privacy Policy

Our commitment to safeguarding your corporate data, business inquiries, and digital privacy across all global operations.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Corporate Identity

The Bell Match Company ("we", "our", or "us"), headquartered in Tamil Nadu, India, respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting the personal and commercial data entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, handling, storage, and protection of information gathered through our official website (bellmatch.com), international export inquiry desks, and customer service channels.

By visiting our website or submitting business inquiries, Request for Quotation (RFQ) forms, or product specification requests, you acknowledge the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to fulfill B2B manufacturing, export quotation, sample fulfillment, and customer service operations:

  • Business Contact Information: Name, business email address, company name, phone number, WhatsApp contact, country of destination, and port of discharge provided during RFQ or general inquiries.
  • Order & Customization Specifications: Target box dimensions, stick count, head colour preferences, splint quality requirements, branding artworks, packaging format specifications, and order volumes.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, device information, operating system, and pages visited, collected automatically to maintain website stability and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by The Bell Match Company is utilized strictly for lawful business purposes, including:

  • Preparing accurate FOB / CIF export quotations and proforma invoices.
  • Coordinating sample dispatch, maritime freight bookings, and customs documentation.
  • Communicating order status updates, lead times, and container shipping advisories.
  • Responding to commercial inquiries submitted via our contact desk or WhatsApp channel.
  • Ensuring compliance with international dangerous goods (DG) maritime transport regulations and export laws.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing fraudulent inquiries.

4. Confidentiality of Client Designs & Private Labels

We understand the high value of proprietary branding. All private label brand names, registered trademarks, bespoke outer box artwork, custom friction patterns, and unique packaging configurations shared with us are treated with strict confidentiality. We do not distribute, license, or repurpose client-owned artwork without express written authorization.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your business or personal data. Information is shared only with vetted third parties strictly as required to fulfill commercial obligations:

  • Logistics & Maritime Freight Carriers: Shipping lines, freight forwarders, and container clearance agents for cargo delivery and bill of lading (B/L) generation.
  • Government & Customs Authorities: Indian customs and destination country port authorities as legally mandated for dangerous goods declarations and Certificate of Origin issuance.
  • Essential Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure (e.g., CDN, email delivery, secure database hosting) operating under rigorous confidentiality and data protection standards.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes standard session cookies and anonymous analytics to ensure proper site navigation, remember user preferences (such as catalog filters), and monitor technical performance. You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, certain interactive website features may be limited.

7. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security protocols to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All web traffic is encrypted via Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Internal database access is restricted to authorized personnel with strict authentication controls.

8. Your Rights & Preferences

You have the right to request access to the personal contact data we hold about you, request corrections or updates, or ask for the deletion of non-transactional contact records from our direct marketing communications. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our data desk using the details below.
